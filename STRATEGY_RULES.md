## ELIGIBILITY (Universe filter — applied monthly)

A stock is **eligible** to be considered if all are true:
1. **5+ years of price history** in the dataset.
2. **Dividend payer**: paid a dividend in **12+ of the last 60 months** (`div_consistency >= 12`).
3. **Not a yield trap**: trailing 12-month yield **< 15%** (cap at 99% if you have a sector cap anyway).
4. **Fundamentals overlay** (for current selection, not historical backtest):
   - P/B > 0 and < 5
   - P/E > 0 and < 30
   - Market Cap >= $1B
   - Beta < 1.5

## SCORE (rank eligible stocks)

Use the **pure_div** score (best IRR) **or** **qual_value** (best Calmar). Pick by your stomach.

**pure_div** (higher CAGR, ~28% MaxDD):
```
score = 0.50 * yield_z + 0.30 * yield_5y_avg_z + 0.20 * div_consistency_z
```

**qual_value** (smoother ride, ~19% MaxDD):
```
score = 0.30 * yield_z + 0.20 * div_growth_z + 0.20 * (-vol_12m_z) + 0.30 * ret_36m_z
```

(`_z` = standardize cross-sectionally within universe each month.)

## SELECTION (Portfolio construction)

- **N = 20 holdings**, equal-weighted.
- **Sector cap = 20%** (max 4 of 20 per sector); **min 7 sectors** represented.
- **Stock allocation = 85%**; **BND floor = 5%** (the rest 10% is rebalance buffer for dip-buys).
- **Hold-bias = 0.5** → existing holding stays if still in top **40** (= N / 0.5).

## REBALANCE CADENCE

- **Annual rebalance** (annual won 92% of top robust runs vs quarterly/monthly — friction matters).
- Re-screen monthly only to **trigger dip-buys**, not to trade.

## BUY rules

You buy a name when **any** of the following happens at the annual rebalance:
1. **New entry**: stock enters top 20 by score and isn't displaced by an existing holding still in top 40.
2. **Sector slot opens** and the new stock has the highest score within its sector.
3. **Dip-buy trigger** (during the year, not just annual):
   - SPY drawdown crosses **-5%** from rolling peak → sell **1/3 of BND**, buy top-ranked underweight names.
   - SPY drawdown crosses **-10%** → sell **1/2 of BND**.
   - SPY drawdown crosses **-20%** → sell **all BND**, full deployment.
4. **Quarterly contribution** ($1,750 every 3 months from the $7k/yr IRA) → use cash to **rebalance toward target** (buy whichever holdings are most underweight); if BND is below 5% floor, top up BND first.

## HOLD rules (bias toward hold)

A current holding **stays in the portfolio** if:
- Its score rank is still **<= 40** (i.e., top 2N), **AND**
- Its sector is not over the 20% cap, **AND**
- It still passes the eligibility filter (still pays a dividend, still 5y+ history).

When in doubt — **hold**.

## SELL rules

You sell a current holding when:
1. **Rank fail**: at annual rebalance, score rank > 40.
2. **Sector overflow**: the sector is over 20% cap AND this is the lowest-scored holding in that sector.
3. **Dividend cut**: stock no longer meets the dividend-consistency filter (paid in <12 of last 60 months).
4. **Yield trap**: trailing 12m yield > 15% **and** stock has dropped >40% in 12 months (signals a doomed dividend).
5. **Snap-back**: total stock allocation > 95% → trim back to 85% target, raise BND back to 5% (sell the lowest-scored holdings proportionally).

## SLEEVE / CASH MANAGEMENT

- **Target**: 85% stocks / 5% BND / 10% buffer.
- **Dividends**: pool to cash (do **not** auto-reinvest in the paying ticker; cash gets deployed at rebalance or dip).
- **IRA contributions**: $1,750 per quarter → cash pool.
- **BND floor**: 5%. Never let it go below.

## CAVEATS

1. **Survivorship bias**: dataset only includes currently-listed tickers. Real returns will be lower than backtested (delisted/bankrupt names absent). The **ranking of strategies** is still informative; the **absolute dollar figures** are inflated.
2. **Fundamentals overlay is current snapshot**, not point-in-time historical (yfinance free tier limit). The historical backtests use price/dividend signals only.
3. **BKE special-dividend mismatch** in cross-validation — flagged but kept; manually verify yield before buying.
4. **Mean-reversion / deep-value** variants (sweep4) showed higher raw IRR (~31%) but much worse drawdowns (-43% to -46%) — not recommended unless you can stomach a 1970s-style decade.
5. **Annual cadence is critical** — quarterly cadence consistently underperforms after friction in the top robust set. Resist the urge to fiddle.
