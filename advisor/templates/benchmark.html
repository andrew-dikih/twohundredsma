Portfolio vs benchmarks

What this page shows

This chart compares your actual portfolio value (measured at each advisor run) against three passive index-only benchmarks, all seeded with the same starting dollar amount as your first recorded run.

Apples-to-apples simulation rules (same cadence as your strategy):

  • Mark-to-market at every run date using ETF prices.
  • Add $1,750 in Jan / Apr / Jul / Oct — split per target weights, just like your IRA contributions.
  • Rebalance back to target weights every February, matching the strategy's annual rebal month.

The benchmarks are:

  • SSO/BND 70/30 — 70% SSO (2× S&P 500 ETF) + 30% BND (US aggregate bonds). The long-term champion among simple two-fund mixes — aggressive but BND dampens drawdowns.
  • VTI/BND 70/30 — 70% VTI (total US stock market) + 30% BND. A textbook three-fund-lite mix. No leverage; lower drawdowns and lower long-term return than SSO/BND.
  • SSO/VTI/BND 50/30/20 — 50% SSO + 30% VTI + 20% BND. A blended "lean aggressive" mix that hedges SSO's volatility with broad market and bonds.

Beating these three with the same dollars deployed at the same cadence is the bar. Anything else isn't a fair comparison.
