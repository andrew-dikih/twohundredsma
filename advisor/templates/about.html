About this strategy

Executive summary (plain English)

This advisor implements the {{ strategy.short_label }} champion strategy — the IRR leader from a 7,200-variation backtest across 18-year, 13-year, and 8-year windows. In simple terms:

  • Own {{ strategy.n_holdings }} dividend-paying stocks, equal-weighted, picked by yield and dividend consistency. Hold ~{{ strategy.stock_w_base_pct }}% of the IRA in those stocks, ~{{ strategy.bnd_floor_pct }}–{{ strategy.bnd_target_pct }}% in BND (US bond ETF) as ballast.
  • Rebalance the full portfolio once a year, in {{ strategy.rebal_month_name }}. That's when the score gets recomputed, losers get cut, winners get topped up. Annual cadence beat quarterly and monthly in 92% of robust runs — friction matters.
  • Add new IRA money every quarter ($1,750 in Jan / Apr / Jul / Oct). Cash goes to whichever holdings are most underweight relative to the target.
  • Buy the dip when SPY corrects. {{ strategy.dip_tier_desc }}. Deploy mode: {{ strategy.dip_deploy }}. {{ strategy.dip_deploy_desc }} This is the only mid-year trading the strategy does.
  • Snap back if stocks balloon. If stock allocation creeps above {{ strategy.snapback_pct }}% (e.g., after a rally), trim back to {{ strategy.stock_w_base_pct }}% and rebuild the BND ballast.
  • Bias toward holding. Existing positions stay if they're still in the top {{ strategy.keep_threshold }} by score, even when not in the top {{ strategy.n_holdings }} — that minimizes turnover and tax drag.

Backtested CAGR: ~30% / yr over 18 years with a max drawdown of about –28%, versus the SSO/BND benchmark at ~20% IRR with a –42% drawdown. See the Benchmark page for live, apples-to-apples comparison once you have a few months of run history.

Eligibility filter (universe screen)

Applied to every ticker, every month, before scoring.

  1. 5+ years of price history in the local dataset.
  2. Dividend payer — paid a dividend in 12+ of the last 60 months.
  3. Not a yield trap — trailing 12-month yield must be below the cap (default 99%, effectively unlimited; the sector cap and snap-back rules handle this in practice).

(The full original strategy doc also describes a fundamentals overlay — P/B < 5, P/E < 30, market cap ≥ $1B, beta < 1.5 — used for current stock selection. The historical backtests rely only on price + dividend signals because point-in-time fundamentals aren't available on the free yfinance tier. The advisor engine in this app uses the backtest-validated price+dividend filter.)

Portfolio construction

  • N = {{ strategy.n_holdings }} holdings, equal-weighted (each ~{{ "%.2f"|format(strategy.stock_w_base_pct / strategy.n_holdings) }}% of the portfolio after stock/bond split).
  • Sector cap = {{ strategy.sector_cap_pct }}% ({{ strategy.sector_cap_mode }}-weighted) — at most {{ strategy.max_per_sector_count }} of the {{ strategy.n_holdings }} names can be in the same sector at rebalance.
  • Minimum {{ strategy.min_sectors }} sectors represented across the portfolio.
  • Target allocation: {{ strategy.stock_w_base_pct }}% stocks / {{ strategy.bnd_floor_pct }}% BND minimum / {{ strategy.bnd_target_pct - strategy.bnd_floor_pct }}% buffer (the buffer is what funds dip-buys).
  • Hold-bias = {{ strategy.hold_bias }} — an existing holding stays in the portfolio if its rank is still ≤ {{ strategy.keep_threshold }} (= N / hold_bias).

Sell rules

A current holding is sold when any of these fire:

  1. Rank fail at the annual rebalance: score rank > {{ strategy.keep_threshold }} (i.e., out of the hold-bias keep zone).
  2. Sector overflow: sector is over the {{ strategy.sector_cap_pct }}% cap AND this is the lowest-scored holding in that sector.
  3. Dividend cut: stock no longer passes the dividend-consistency filter (<12 dividends in last 60 months).
  4. Snap-back trim: stock allocation > {{ strategy.snapback_pct }}% triggers proportional trim of all holdings back to {{ strategy.stock_w_base_pct }}%, rebuilding BND.
  5. Reallocation at annual rebalance to bring overweight holdings back to equal weight.

Cash & sleeve management

  • Dividends pool to cash — they are not auto-reinvested in the paying ticker. Cash is deployed at the next rebalance, dip, or contribution event.
  • IRA contributions: $1,750 per quarter = $7,000/year (current Roth IRA limit assumption for one earner).
  • BND floor: {{ strategy.bnd_floor_pct }}%. Topped up first whenever cash is deployed via contribution.

Caveats (worth re-reading)

  1. Survivorship bias: the historical dataset only contains currently-listed tickers. Backtested dollar figures are inflated; real-world returns will be lower. The ranking of strategies vs. the SSO/BND benchmark is still informative.
  2. Fundamentals overlay is a current snapshot, not point-in-time historical data. The advisor's stock filter relies on price + dividend signals only.
  3. Annual cadence is non-negotiable. Quarterly rebalancing consistently underperformed in robust testing once friction was modeled. Resist the urge to fiddle.
  4. The dip-buy thresholds are crossings of rolling SPY peak, not absolute drops — once a tier (e.g. {{ ((strategy.dip_tier[0][0]) * 100)|int }}%) fires, it won't fire again until SPY makes a new peak and re-crosses that level.
  5. AI review (if enabled) is informational only. The orders the advisor produces are deterministic; the AI overlay flags things to consider but does not change the trade list.
